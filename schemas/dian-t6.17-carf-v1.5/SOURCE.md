# Schema provenance

🌐 **English** · [Español](SOURCE.es.md)

XSDs extracted from the appendices of **Technical Annex T6.17 "CARF XML v1.5"** of Resolution 000240 of 24 Dec 2025 of DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales, Colombia's tax and customs authority)
(https://www.dian.gov.co/normatividad/Normatividad/Anexo%202%20-%20Resoluci%C3%B3n%20000240%20de%202025.pdf).
They are structurally identical to the OECD CARF XML Schema (namespaces `urn:oecd:ties:carf:v1`, `urn:oecd:ties:carfstf:v5`, `urn:oecd:ties:isocarftypes:v1`).

- CARFXML_v1.5.xsd
- oecdcarftypes_v5.0.xsd
- isocarftypes_v1.1.xsd

Check against the official version before each reporting season.
